Grüezi! Meet Regine

🖼️Portrait goes here

Regine Bertschinger teaches German and English at the Kantonsschule Zürcher Oberland (KZO) in the Zürcher Oberland. That means she's fluent in Goethe and Shakespeare, and she can spot a misplaced comma in either language.

Teaching is only part of it. She sits on internal and external committees, leads Fachwochen (subject weeks), and organises the ski tour weeks, where students find out what "uphill" really means.

When the Swiss Women's Strike (Frauenstreik) came around, she was the media representative for the KZO women's group.
